AirSave Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

AirSave saves web content to your Google Drive on your explicit request.

Data collected: None. AirSave does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to its own servers.

Google Drive access: AirSave requests permission to upload files solely for the save action you initiate by right-clicking. It does not read, modify, or delete existing Drive files.

Third parties: AirSave does not share any data with third parties.
